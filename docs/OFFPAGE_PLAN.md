# Trimly: Off-Page & Local SEO Master Plan (India)

**Target Market:** India (Metros + Emerging Grooming Hubs: Indore, Bhopal, Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad)  
**Core Objective:** Drive authoritative local citations, dominate Google Map Pack (Local 3-Pack), establish ethical backlink pipelines, and scale organic B2B salon partner acquisition.

---

## 1. Google Business Profile (GBP) Blueprint

For every partner salon onboarded onto Trimly, an optimized Google Business Profile is the #1 lever to capture hyper-local "near me" traffic.

### 1.1 Category Hierarchy
- **Primary Category:** `Barber shop` (for male-focused grooming lounges) OR `Hair salon` (for unisex/modern styling studios).
- **Secondary Categories (Select 2-4):**
  - `Beauty salon`
  - `Facial spa`
  - `Waxing hair removal service`
  - `Stylist`

### 1.2 Exact GBP Attribute Checklist
Ensure these attributes are explicitly toggled on in the GBP manager:
- **Appointments & Planning:**
  - `Online appointments:` **YES** → Enter canonical Trimly URL: `https://trimly.in/barbers/[city]/[slug]`
  - `Appointment required:` **YES**
- **Amenities:**
  - `Air conditioning:` **YES**
  - `Wi-Fi:` **YES / Free**
  - `Restroom:` **YES**
- **Payments:**
  - `UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm):` **YES**
  - `Credit cards / Debit cards:` **YES**
  - `Cash only:` **NO**
- **Crowd & Identity:**
  - `LGBTQ+ friendly:` **YES**
  - `Family friendly:` **YES**

### 1.3 Photo Optimization Guidelines (Google Lens & Vision AI)
Google Vision AI scans uploaded GBP images for tools, chair equipment, and signage. Partner salons should upload:
1. **Storefront Exterior (3-5 photos):** Clear daylight street view showing the shop board name matching the GBP name exactly. Helps Google Maps Street View validation.
2. **Interior & Workstations (5-10 photos):** Barber chairs, styling mirrors, wash stations, clean aesthetic lighting.
3. **Hygiene & Equipment (2-4 photos):** UV sterilizer boxes, sanitized razor setups, clean disposable capes.
4. **Transparent Rate Card / Menu Board (1-2 photos):** Clear INR price display.
5. **Team in Action (3-5 photos):** Stylists actively grooming clients (with client consent).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **EXIF Geo-Tagging:** Encourage salons to take photos with mobile GPS enabled directly at the shop location so image EXIF metadata contains local latitude/longitude coordinates matching the address.

### 1.4 Post-Appointment Review Generation Workflow
To build consistent 5-star ratings without spamming:
1. **Trigger:** Triggered automatically 45 minutes after appointment status is updated to `completed` in Trimly.
2. **Channel:** Fast2SMS / WhatsApp / Email alert.
3. **Copy Blueprint:**
   > *"Hi [Customer Name], thank you for visiting [Salon Name] today via Trimly! How was your haircut with [Stylist Name]? Tap here to leave a quick 30-second review and help fellow neighbors find great stylists: https://trimly.in/barbers/[city]/[slug]#reviews"*
4. **Google Review Handoff:** For 5-star ratings on Trimly, present an optional one-click button: *"Share this review on Google Maps to support [Salon Name]!"*

## 2. Indian Local Citations & Directory Listings

NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency across Indian web directories reinforces geographic authority in Google's Knowledge Graph.

### 2.1 NAP Standardization Rule
Always use the exact registered shop name and standardized address format:
- **Format:** `[Shop Name], [Shop No/Plot], [Market/Landmark], [Locality], [City], [State] - [Pincode]`
- **Example:** `Classic Cuts Lounge, Shop 14, Opposite Treasure Island Mall, MG Road, Indore, Madhya Pradesh - 452001`
- **Phone:** Standardized `+91` format.

### 2.2 Priority 1 Directories (High Domain Authority in India)

| Platform | Domain Authority (DA) | Listing Type | Key Actions |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Justdial** | 84 | Free Business Profile | Add shop name, primary categories, website URL pointing to Trimly canonical page, verified phone number. Avoid paid ad lock-in. |
| **Sulekha** | 76 | Local Services | Create listing under "Beauty Parlours & Salons", link to Trimly booking profile. |
| **Magicpin** | 68 | Hyperlocal Discovery | Claim salon venue profile, upload menu photos, add "Book Online" link. |
| **Nearbuy** | 65 | Local Lifestyle Deals | Partner profile linking to Trimly slot calendar. |
| **IndiaMart** | 88 | B2B Directory | Create company profile for Trimly as a salon appointment marketplace. |

### 2.3 Priority 2 Directories & Tech Platforms
1. **Apple Business Connect (Apple Maps):** Vital for iPhone users across Indian metros. Register salon locations with appointment links pointing to `https://trimly.in/barbers/[city]/[slug]`.
2. **Bing Places for Business:** Sync directly with Google Business Profile.
3. **Facebook Local Page & Instagram Location:** Create an official location tag for each partner salon on Instagram. When customers post grooming reels/stories, they tag the location.
4. **Foursquare / Swarm:** Feeds location data into Twitter, Uber, and navigation apps.

## 4. Ethical Backlink Acquisition Plan for India

Google devalues automated PBNs and spammy directory links. Trimly will execute four high-intent, ethical link-building tactics:

### 4.1 City Lifestyle & Culture Portals
Indian urban lifestyle publications frequently publish curated lists ("Best Men's Salons in Bandra", "Top Barbers in Indore for Fades").
- **Target Publishers:**
  - *LBB (Little Black Book):* Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad, Kolkata.
  - *Curly Tales:* Food, travel & urban grooming recommendations.
  - *WhatsHot:* City guides for youth lifestyle.
  - *So City:* Delhi-NCR hyper-local curation.
- **Outreach Angle:** Provide journalists with exclusive data from Trimly: *"Trimly's 2026 Grooming Index: Average haircut prices and top-rated barbers across Indore, Bhopal & Mumbai"*.

### 4.2 College & University Campus Guides
Indian college students (18-24) are the highest-frequency male haircut consumers (every 2-3 weeks).
- **Campaign:** Partner with student councils and campus blogs at major institutions (e.g., IIT Indore, IIM Indore, MANIT Bhopal, NMIMS Mumbai, Christ University Bengaluru).
- **Asset:** "The Ultimate Student Grooming Guide: Best Budget Barbers Near [Campus Name] with Transparent Student Rates".
- **Link Placement:** Student discount resource pages and local campus accommodation guides (.ac.in or student union domains).

### 4.3 Salon Owner Digital Badges ("Proud Trimly Partner")
Every verified salon receives a digital embed badge for their personal website, Linktree, or Instagram bio:
- `<a href="https://trimly.in/barbers/[city]/[slug]"><img src="https://trimly.in/badge-verified.svg" alt="Book on Trimly" /></a>`
- This creates authentic, branded, do-follow links from hundreds of salon websites directly to their Trimly money pages.

### 4.4 Grooming Influencer Barters (YouTube & Instagram)
- Target nano- and micro-influencers (5k - 50k followers) in target cities (e.g. Indore fashion vloggers, Mumbai grooming creators).
- Barter offer: Complimentary ₹1,500 luxury grooming package at a top-rated Trimly partner salon.
- Deliverable: Video reel showing the booking process on Trimly ("Found this hidden gem barber on Trimly with instant slot booking"), linking to `https://trimly.in/barbers/[city]` in the bio/description.

## 5. Review Generation & Reputation Defense SOP

1. **Negative Review Shield:** When a customer rates an appointment 1 or 2 stars on Trimly, an automated alert triggers to the salon owner and Trimly admin within 15 minutes, allowing immediate service recovery before an external 1-star review is posted on Google Maps.
2. **Review Response Policy:** All partner barbers are coached on the 3-step review response framework:
   - *Acknowledge & Thank:* Use customer's name and service.
   - *Mention Service & City:* Reinforces local keywords naturally (*"Thank you Rahul! Our team at Classic Cuts Indore loved giving you that clean drop fade."*).
   - *Re-invite:* Friendly closing statement.
